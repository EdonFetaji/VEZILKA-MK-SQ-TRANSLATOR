Experiment report · NLLB-200-distilled-600M · Macedonian ↔ Albanian

MK–SQ Gazette Experiments

Службен весник ↔ Gazeta Zyrtare

Fine-tuning a 600M-parameter NLLB model on the bilingual Official Gazette of North Macedonia, with and without the Verbis terminology dictionary. E01 trains on the Gazette plus Verbis term and definition pairs. E02 is the control: the same run with no Verbis data. Both are compared with the zero-shot base model (E00), and E01 against E02 shows what the dictionary adds.

SUMMARY

What E01 and E02 show

  • Large in-domain gain from both runs. On the full Gazette test set (10,174 pairs) BLEU roughly doubled: 27.74 → 54.02 (E01) and 54.28 (E02) MK→SQ, 29.63 → 63.64 (E01) and 63.80 (E02) SQ→MK. E01's gains over the base model have p = 0.001.
  • Verbis adds nothing on Gazette test BLEU. E01 and E02 are within 0.26 BLEU and 0.14 chrF++ of each other on the Gazette test, with E02 slightly ahead in both directions.
  • Verbis reduces general-domain forgetting. Both runs lose quality on FLORES+ and NTREX-128, but E02 loses more: −4.6 to −7.9 BLEU against −2.5 to −5.0 for E01. E01 is 2.1 to 3.5 BLEU ahead of E02 on every out-of-domain set and direction.
  • The E01 gain is not just memorised templates. 48.6% of test pairs are templates or near-duplicates of training sentences; on the novel half E01 still gains +22.37 BLEU MK→SQ and +28.66 SQ→MK.
  • E01 terminology reaches the human rate. Term Success Rate rose from 56.3% to 75.8% (MK→SQ) and from 55.2% to 66.9% (SQ→MK). Rare terms still lag, most of all SQ→MK (51.6% vs 78.9%).
  • Not yet measured for E02. Significance of E01 vs E02, the overlap breakdown, Term Success Rate and number fidelity need the analysis step on E02's translations. Without E02's TSR we cannot yet say whether Verbis improves terminology.

Verbis terms and digits in the output

Term Success Rate counts a Verbis term occurrence in the source as a success when the hypothesis contains the listed target term (prefix match on the first max(4, length − 2) characters). 05 · TRAINING

One epoch, 4,115 steps per run

Full-parameter fine-tuning on one RTX 5080, batch 8 × 16 accumulation = 128 examples per step, Adafactor at 1e-4 with 1,000 warm-up steps and linear decay. Dev scores are greedy decoding on 500 Gazette dev sentences per direction, used only to select the checkpoint.

06 · DEPLOYMENT

CTranslate2 int8 matches the full-precision model

The fine-tuned model was exported to CTranslate2 with int8 weights. CTranslate2 could not use the GPU on this machine, so the int8 model ran on 16 CPU threads.

int8 minus bf16

E01 only. 2 of 12 differences reach p < 0.05; the largest is 0.23 BLEU. E02's int8 export behaves the same way: within 0.44 BLEU of its bf16 model on every set (see the table in section 01).

07 · DATA

What the model was trained on

Any training pair whose Macedonian or Albanian side appears in a held-out set (Gazette dev or test, FLORES+, NTREX-128) was removed before training. Each remaining pair was used in both directions; examples longer than 192 tokens were dropped, not truncated.

Training mix

E02 drops both Verbis parts and keeps everything else identical, so E01 vs E02 isolates the dictionary's contribution. Verbis is still used in E02 to measure Term Success Rate.

  • One seed per run. No variance across seeds is available, so small E01 vs E02 differences cannot be separated from seed noise.
  • E02 is partly analysed. Only test-set scores and three dev evaluations are in. Significance, overlap, terminology, number fidelity and the training log are still to come.
  • Formulaic overlap. Full-test scores overstate generalisation; the novel subset is the fairer number.
  • TSR is a heuristic. The reference itself uses the listed Verbis target only 73.6% (MK→SQ) and 67.1% (SQ→MK) of the time. Rare-term rates rest on 220 and 128 occurrences.
  • Data revisions not logged by E01. The Gazette, FLORES+ and NTREX revisions were inferred from timing; E02 pins them, and E02's base-model scores match E01's exactly on all six test-set directions.
  • E02 training was resumed. It crashed (a bug in the dev-curve logging, not in training) at the step-2,000 dev evaluation and resumed from checkpoint 1,000; the step-1,000 dev row comes from commit bd3c440, everything after from ccd58f9.
  • Dev vs test decoding. Dev used greedy decoding on 500 sentences per direction; test used beam 4.
  • Verbis licence. Redistribution needs the Agency's permission; the data stay private and no entry is shown here.
